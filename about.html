Our Story

We started Toothent because too many people dread the dentist, and honestly, we don’t blame them. So we built the kind of clinic we’d want to visit ourselves: calm, honest and kind.

Whether it’s your little one’s very first check-up or a smile you’ve wanted to fix for years, we’ll listen first, explain everything in plain words and never push you into anything.

Our Mission

We want every family in Kannur to get gentle, honest and modern dental care. We explain your treatment in simple words. We tell you the cost before we start. And we make sure you leave feeling cared for.

Our Vision
