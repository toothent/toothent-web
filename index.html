Been Putting
It Off?

We get it, life gets busy. But most of these small things are quicker, simpler and cheaper to fix when you catch them early.

That twinge with ice cream

Sensitivity to cold or sweet things is often an early sign of decay or worn enamel.

Pink when you brush

Bleeding gums usually mean early gum disease, which is very treatable at this stage.

Hiding a chipped tooth

A chip is usually a quick fix, and treating it early protects the rest of the tooth.

Teeth that overlap

Crowded teeth are harder to clean. Aligners or braces can help at almost any age.

It’s been a while

No lecture, we promise. A dental check-up and cleaning is the easiest way to start again.
