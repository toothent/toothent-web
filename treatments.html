01 / Aligners

Aligners

Want straighter teeth without metal braces? Clear aligners are a series of nearly invisible trays that gently move your teeth, and you can take them out to eat and brush.

  • Nearly invisible
  • Removable for meals and brushing
  • No wires or brackets
  • Suitable for teens and adults

02 / Implants

Dental Implants

Missing a tooth makes it harder to eat, talk and smile. A dental implant replaces the whole tooth, root and all, so it looks and feels just like your own. Looking for dental implants in Kannur? We do them right here at our clinic.

  • Single or multiple teeth
  • Implant-supported bridges
  • Implant-retained dentures
  • Long-lasting results

04 / Root Canal

Root Canal Treatment

Bad toothache keeping you up at night? A root canal cleans out the infection inside your tooth, stops the pain and saves your tooth. We numb the area first, so it feels a lot like getting a filling. Need root canal treatment in Taliparamba? We can help.

  • Relieves tooth pain
  • Saves your natural tooth
  • Gentle, modern techniques
  • Crown to protect the tooth

05 / Crowns & Bridges

Crowns & Bridges

A crown protects a weak or broken tooth, and a bridge fills the gap where a tooth is missing. Both are made to match the colour and shape of your own teeth.

  • Crowns for damaged teeth
  • Bridges for missing teeth
  • Tooth-coloured ceramic options
  • Strong for everyday chewing
